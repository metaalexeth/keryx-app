Keryx: privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Keryx is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @metaalexeth to upload that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and visibility.
  • What it stores: an access token kept on the owner's own computer. No data about any other person is collected, stored or shared.
  • Who uses it: only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: through the channel youtube.com/@metaalexeth.

Keryx's use of information received from Google APIs follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy.
